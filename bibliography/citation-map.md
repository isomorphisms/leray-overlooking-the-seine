## Contact topology / Seifert's conjecture

- [Etnyre & Ghrist (2000), *Contact topology and hydrodynamics I: Beltrami fields and the Seifert conjecture*](https://etnyre.math.gatech.edu/preprints/papers/hydro.pdf): up to rescaling, nonvanishing rotational Beltrami fields correspond to Reeb fields. Via the Weinstein conjecture, this gives periodic-orbit results for certain steady Euler flows.
- [Part II: *Solid tori* (2002)](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0143385702000408): closed orbits in real-analytic steady Euler flows on solid tori.
- [Part III: *Knotted orbits* (2000)](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-00-02651-9): realizes many knot and link types among periodic orbits of a steady Euler flow on a 3-sphere.

**Disambiguation:** the Seifert conjecture here concerns **closed orbits of vector fields**. It is not a paper about Seifert-fibered manifolds, nor is it a citation in OpenAI's manuscript. The contact-topological setting is steady **inviscid Euler flow**, not the forced finite-time Navier–Stokes construction.
